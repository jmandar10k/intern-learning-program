# Go/no-go criteria

Shared with the intern on Day 1. The four-week review at the end of Week 4 is a **formal
go/no-go checkpoint** for continuation.

## What the decision is based on

The decision rests on three things:

1. **Demonstrated learning**: what you can now do that you could not on Day 1, shown in the
   check-ins, Week 3 reviews and Week 4 tasks.
2. **Initiative**: how you handled being stuck, the questions you asked, the ideas you proposed.
3. **Readiness to contribute**: whether you could take a scoped piece of real work next week
   and deliver it with normal mentor support.

It is **not** based on how much routine work you completed.

## Evidence used

| Source | Feeds into |
|---|---|
| Rubric scores (5 dimensions, 1–4) | All three |
| Week 4 task scores ([task-scoring-sheet](../assessment/task-scoring-sheet.md)) | Learning, technical quality, readiness |
| Demo and Q&A ([final-demo-qa-guide](../assessment/final-demo-qa-guide.md)) | Learning, communication, readiness |
| Week 3 review submissions | Learning, technical quality |
| Weekly check-ins and progress log | Learning trajectory, initiative |
| GenAI disclosures | GenAI judgment |

## Decision rules

| Outcome | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Go** | Weighted overall ≥ 2.75, **and** no dimension scored 1, **and** at least two of the three Week 4 tasks meet their acceptance criteria (Task 3 may be partial if the design note and failure analysis are sound). |
| **Go with conditions** | Weighted overall 2.25–2.74, **or** one dimension scored 1 except GenAI judgment, **with** a clear upward trend across the weekly check-ins. Conditions are written down with a 2–4 week follow-up date. |
| **No-go** | Weighted overall < 2.25, **or** two or more dimensions scored 1, **or** a breach of the data rules in the [GenAI usage policy](genai-usage-policy.md), **or** submitted work the intern cannot explain. |

The mentor may depart from these rules only with a written justification in the
[four-week review](../assessment/four-week-review-template.md), reviewed by a second reviewer.

## Process

1. Week 4, Day 5 morning: demo with Q&A (45–60 min).
2. Mentor completes the task scoring sheet and rubric scores the same day.
3. A second reviewer reads the evidence and scores independently; differences of 2+ points on
   any dimension are discussed and resolved.
4. Week 4, Day 5 afternoon: four-week review meeting with the intern. The decision and its
   reasons are shared verbally and in writing.

## Fairness

- You know the criteria from Day 1 (this file).
- Weekly check-ins tell you where you stand, so the final review holds no surprises.
- Every score is tied to written evidence.
