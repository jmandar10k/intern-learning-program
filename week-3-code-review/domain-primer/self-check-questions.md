# Domain primer: self-check questions

**Ungraded.** Answer briefly in [progress-log/week-3-log.md](../../progress-log/week-3-log.md);
they are discussed at the Week 3 check-in. A sentence or two (or a quick calculation) each.

1. A price goes 50 → 55 → 44. What is the total simple return? Why is adding the two period
   returns wrong?
2. Why do log returns add over time while simple returns do not?
3. Unadjusted daily data shows a stock falling from 300 to 150 overnight with no news. What
   probably happened, and what would it do to a backtest?
4. Capital 50,000, risk 2% per trade, entry 40.00, stop 38.00. How many shares?
5. A signal uses today's close. What is the earliest realistic time you could trade on it?
6. Explain in-sample vs out-of-sample using ML vocabulary.
7. Sketch walk-forward analysis for 6 years of data.
8. A strategy's gross edge is 0.08% per trade and round-trip costs are 0.10%. What happens?
9. 5,000 → 8,000 over 4 years. What is the CAGR (roughly)?
10. Win rate 35%, average win 300, average loss 100. Expectancy per trade?
11. Daily volatility is 2%. What is the approximate annual volatility?
12. Equity: 100, 140, 105, 150, 120. What is the max drawdown?
13. Why can two strategies with the same Sharpe ratio feel very different to hold?
14. Name the ML concept behind look-ahead bias, survivorship bias and data snooping.
15. A backtest shows 15 trades with an 80% win rate and a Sharpe of 3. List your first three
    questions.
