How the review exercises work, and their rules: [README.md](README.md).

## Pace

About 3 hours per exercise. If you are stuck for more than 45 minutes on one exercise, write
down what you have tried, move to the next one, and come back. Raise it at office hours:
asking a good question after trying is scored positively under "Initiative".

## Week 3 check-in (Day 15)

Focus: **quality of review reasoning, initiative, and discussion of the primer's self-check
questions.** Bring your submissions and your primer answers.
